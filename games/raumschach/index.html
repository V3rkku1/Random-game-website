Raumschach

Rules5×5×5 board, levels A (bottom) to E (top). Rook moves along 1 axis, bishop along 2 axes at once, unicorn (spiked orb) along all 3. Queen combines all three; king steps one square in any of 26 directions. Knight leaps 2+1 in any two axes. Pawns step forward or toward the enemy's level and capture diagonally in either plane; they promote to queen on the far rank of the far level. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
